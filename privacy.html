Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Winpost ("Winpost", "we", "us" or "our") handles information when you use the Winpost mobile application.

1. Information we collect

Email address

Email addresses may be collected for authorized moderator or administrative accounts used to access protected Winpost moderation functionality.

User content

If you submit a giveaway, report content, or send other information through features provided by Winpost, we may process the information you provide, such as URLs, notes, report reasons and related submission content.

User and device identifiers

Winpost may use account identifiers, installation identifiers, device-related identifiers and push notification tokens when needed to provide app functionality, synchronize preferences, protect the service and deliver notifications.

Purchase information

If you subscribe to Winpost Plus, subscription status and related purchase information may be processed to unlock paid features and keep your subscription entitlement synchronized. Payments are processed by Apple through the App Store. Winpost does not receive your full payment card details.

Product interaction and preferences

Winpost may process app preferences and interaction information needed to personalize and operate the service, including followed creators, catalog country, language, notification preferences and Winpost Plus entitlement status.

2. How we use information

  • Provide and maintain Winpost features.
  • Authenticate authorized moderator accounts.
  • Process user submissions and reports.
  • Deliver requested notifications and reminders.
  • Personalize supported parts of the app.
  • Verify and maintain subscription access.
  • Protect Winpost from abuse, fraud or unauthorized access.
  • Support users and maintain service reliability.

3. Third-party service providers

Winpost relies on service providers that may process information on our behalf in order to operate the app.

  • Supabase may be used for backend infrastructure, authentication, database storage and server-side functionality.
  • Apple provides App Store purchases, StoreKit and Apple Push Notification service functionality.
  • Winpost may open or reference content hosted on third-party platforms such as Instagram, TikTok and YouTube. Your use of those services is governed by their own privacy policies and terms.

4. Advertising and tracking

Winpost does not use the information described in this policy for cross-company advertising tracking and does not sell personal information to data brokers.

5. Data retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to operate Winpost, provide the relevant feature, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, prevent abuse and enforce our agreements.

6. Data security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect information handled by Winpost. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Your choices and requests

You may disable notifications in iOS Settings and change supported preferences inside Winpost.

8. Children

9. International processing

Information may be processed in countries other than the country where you live, depending on the infrastructure and service providers used by Winpost.
